About HASK

Human Resource & Administration Sheikhupura

HASK (HR & Admin Sheikhupura) is a premier collective initiative designed to unite HR and Administration professionals across Sheikhupura. Our platform serves as a bridge, bringing together experts working within the city and those representing Sheikhupura globally, all under one dedicated umbrella.

We aim to empower Sheikhupura's professional community by fostering collaboration, sharing best practices, and creating opportunities for growth and recognition on a national and international level.

🎯

Our Mission

To unite HR & Admin professionals of Sheikhupura under one strong, supportive network.

👁️

Our Vision

To become the most trusted HR & Admin community of Punjab, representing Sheikhupura globally.

💼

Our Values

Integrity, collaboration, continuous learning, and professional excellence.

🌟

Our Goal

Creating opportunities and recognition for HR talent of Sheikhupura.
